# SpecSentry — Demo Script

Opening line for an estimator: **"You read a three-hundred-page spec three
times before every bid. SpecSentry reads it once and shows its work."**

7 scenes, about two and a half minutes narrated. Everything shown is a real
extraction from a real PDF, a water-tank coating spec processed live.

---

## Scene 1 — `01-home` · Specs home
**Say:** "This is SpecSentry, built for industrial coatings estimators. The header is the contract: it cites or it flags, it never guesses, and it never prices. Drop a spec PDF here and it reads every section."

## Scene 2 — `02-detail` · The extraction
**Say:** "Here's a tank rehab spec, processed live. The scoreboard says what happened: twelve requirements extracted, two flagged for human review, zero price-guard catches. And the flags are the point. This spec wrote its blast standards in longhand, so instead of pretending to recognize them, it flagged both for you to confirm."

## Scene 3 — `03-coat-systems` · Coat systems, page-cited
**Say:** "Both coat systems came out structured: the interior immersion system and the exterior system, primer, intermediate, and finish, with the mil thicknesses as chips and a page citation on every card. If a number's in here, you can put your finger on the exact page it came from."

## Scene 4 — `04-env-limits` · Environmental limits
**Say:** "Environmental limits are pulled verbatim. Eighty-five percent humidity ceiling, five degrees above dew point, the ambient window. These are the clauses that blow up a bid when they're missed, because they mean dehumidification equipment and schedule risk."

## Scene 5 — `05-hold-points` · Hold points
**Say:** "Hold points, same treatment. Prep sign-off before primer, DFT verification between coats, holiday testing before disinfection. Each one is inspector time and schedule you need in the number."

## Scene 6 — `06-deliverables` · The three deliverables
**Say:** "Three files come out. A page-cited requirements sheet. An inspection package, watermarked draft. And the cost-driver checklist, where every price cell is deliberately blank. It surfaces the drivers, confined space entry, containment, dehumidification, and you price them. Your labor rates and your margins never enter the software."

## Scene 7 — `07-dark` · Close
**Say:** "That's SpecSentry. Hours of spec reading turned into a page-cited checklist you can verify in minutes. It reads the words, you make the bid."

---

## Q&A ammunition

- **"My estimators will re-read the spec anyway."** Maybe on bid one. The wedge
  is the flags: it refuses to guess and every extraction is page-cited, so
  checking it takes minutes, not hours. The pilot measures hours per bid,
  before and after.
- **"A missed clause costs six figures."** Which is why it never judges. It
  extracts verbatim with citations, the validators flag anything nonstandard,
  and the cost-driver checklist is deliberately over-inclusive.
- **"Why doesn't it do takeoff too?"** Takeoff is crowded, Beam, Togal,
  On-Screen. SpecSentry reads the words, not the drawings. That's the half
  nobody bundled.
